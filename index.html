Neoliberal Values

An exploratory political values quiz built around disagreements within and around the r/neoliberal community. The axes are provisional and intended to be recalibrated from respondent data.

Click here to start!

What is Neoliberal Values?

This test measures seven provisional political dimensions that appear useful for describing disagreement within r/neoliberal. The present scores are exploratory rather than population-calibrated. As response data accumulates, the questions, axes, and eventual respondent blocs can be revised.

What are the seven axes?

Each axis contains two opposing tendencies. Neither endpoint is intended as a party label or a recommendation.
